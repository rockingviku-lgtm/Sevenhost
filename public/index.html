Frequently asked questions

Can I buy hosting today?

Not yet. Real hosting needs a paid server that we have not launched. We will not take money for something that is not running.

What is “Static Site Setup”?

For websites made of HTML, CSS and JavaScript. We help you deploy it to your own free Cloudflare account, so the account and the site belong to you.

Will you host my WordPress or PHP site for free?

No. Free static hosting cannot run PHP or WordPress. Those need a real server, which is a later stage.

Why are limits not “unlimited”?

Every server has limits. We will publish real numbers and enforce them fairly instead of hiding them in fine print.

Do you register domains?

Not yet. When we do, it will be through an authorised registrar or reseller programme, and prices will be shown before checkout.

What happens to the details I submit?

Only what you type into the waitlist or contact form, used to reply to you about SevenHost. Please never send passwords, card numbers or secrets through any form here.

Is there a service guarantee?

No guarantee while we are pre-launch. Any uptime promise will be written down only after monitoring proves we can keep it.
